# NexFlow Landing Page — UX Writing Issues & Recommendations

| # | Issue | Why it's a problem | Suggestion |
|---|-------|--------------------|------------|
| 1 | **"إدارة أعمالك بنموذج ذكي مع NexFlow"** | "بنموذج ذكي" is vague and doesn't explain what the product actually does. | Try something like **"نظام كاشير ونقاط بيع لإدارة محلك من مكان واحد"**. Focus on the job to be done rather than "ذكي". |
| 2 | **"كل ده في مكان واحد ومن أي مكان"** | "كل ده" is ambiguous. The user doesn't know what "ده" refers to. | Be explicit: **"راقب مبيعاتك، مخزونك، عملائك وأرباحك من مكان واحد، ومن أي جهاز"**. |
| 3 | **Stats ("٩٩.٩٪ وقت تشغيل النظام", "+٢٠٠٠ فاتورة يومياً")** | Uptime is technical and could be unverifiable on a marketing page. "فاتورة يومياً" also lacks context (per merchant? total?). | Either remove uptime or rephrase to customer-centric proof (e.g. **"يعمل بدون توقف"** with a disclaimer), and clarify the source of other stats. |

### 2) CTAs & Conversion Flow

| # | Issue | Why it's a problem | Suggestion |
|---|-------|--------------------|------------|
| 4 | **Mixed CTAs** | "ابدأ تجربتك دلوقتي" (Hero), "ابدأ دلوقتي" (Navbar), "اشترك..." (Pricing), "امتلك النظام" (Lifetime). The action differs but isn't explained. | Pick one primary action for "trial/demo" (since it's WhatsApp-first, not self-serve) e.g. **"احجز عرض تجريبي"** across the site. Use **"اشترك الآن"** only when going to a purchase action, and clarify what clicking does. |
| 5 | **"اكتشف المميزات"** | It's a scroll link, but reads like a vague CTA next to a primary action. | Consider **"شوف المميزات"** or just rely on scrolling. Alternatively make it more specific like **"تعرف على المميزات"**. |
| 6 | **WhatsApp-first flow isn't explicit** | Users don't know if clicking "ابدأ" will start a chat, schedule a demo, or sell them something. | Add microcopy near CTAs like **"هيتم تحويلك للواتساب لعرض تجريبي مجانًا"** or state **"بدون التزام"** more explicitly (you already do this in CTA, expand it). |

### 3) Pricing & FAQs

| # | Issue | Why it's a problem | Suggestion |
|---|-------|--------------------|------------|
| 7 | **"بسدد مرة واحدة — وخلاص!" (lifetime)** | Very casual/unclear. Doesn't communicate value or reassurance. | Try **"دفعة واحدة للأبد — بدون اشتراك"** or **"امتلكه مدى الحياة بدون تجديد"**. |
| 8 | **"وفّر ١٬٢٠٠ ج.م — يعني ٥٠٠ج في الشهر بس!"** | The math is a bit confusing (6000 vs 7200). It's also a bit pushy. | Just state **"توفير ١,٢٠٠ ج.م مقارنة بالاشتراك الشهري"** which is clearer. |
| 9 | **"بباقة مدى الحياة بتتقسط على دفعتين لو حابب" (FAQ)** | Important info that's buried in FAQ, not visible on the pricing card. | Surface installment info on the lifetime card if it's available, or at least in the payment note. |
| 10 | **"عرض تجريبي كامل (Demo)" (FAQ)** | Mixing English "Demo" unnecessarily in Arabic copy. | Say **"عرض تجريبي كامل"** or **"نسخة تجريبية"**. |

### 4) Tone, Grammar & Consistency

| # | Issue | Why it's a problem | Suggestion |
|---|-------|--------------------|------------|
| 11 | **"فرّقنا جنب النظام القديم وهتعرف الفرق بنفسك" (CTA)** | Awkward grammar ("فرّقنا جنب..."). Unclear phrasing. | Try **"جرب الفرق بين NexFlow والنظام القديم بنفسك"** or **"شوف الفرق بنفسك بعد ما تجرب النظام"**. |
| 12 | **"نقل بياناتك من أي نظام تاني"** | "تاني" is colloquial Egyptian Arabic which is fine, but inconsistent with more formal phrasing elsewhere. | Either commit fully to Egyptian Arabic throughout, or use "آخر". Right now it's a mix that feels unpolished. |
| 13 | **"ذكاء مصري ١٠٠٪… بيخلي أرباحك تتكلم 📈" (footer/README)** | Catchy, but abstract. Doesn't say what the product does. | It's nice for brand personality, but consider pairing with a concrete benefit or moving it to a tagline that's separate from the value prop. |
| 14 | **"كاشير" vs "نقاط بيع"** | Used interchangeably (e.g. "نظام كاشير", "نقاط بيع", "POS"). Both correct, but can be confusing. | Pick one primary term for the hero (e.g. **"نظام نقاط بيع وكاشير"**) and stick to it, or use them to describe different aspects. |
| 15 | **"بدون بطاقة ائتمانية"** | Misleading for a WhatsApp sales flow. There's no self-serve checkout, so this implies a trial signup form. | Rephrase to **"بدون التزام"** (which you already use) or **"جرب مجانًا بدون التزام"**. This matches the actual flow better. |

> **Note:** The overall copy is very strong for an Egyptian SMB audience. Most of these are small tweaks.
